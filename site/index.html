How a night here goes

Three steps. None of them involve a quiz about your attachment style.

  1. Walk in

    Make a profile: your name, your usual order and one photo that looks like you.

  2. Look around

    Everyone at the bar is on one page. Give each person a verdict: another round, or call it a night.

  3. Take a booth

    Message anyone directly. Verdicts are public. Conversations are not.
